Practical Curriculum

What Students Learn

Fourteen core competencies taught through real-world diagnostics, enterprise tools, and actionable workflows.

Keyword Research

Discovering low-competition, high-intent transactional search terms that actually convert.

Search Intent

Dissecting SERP features to match informational, commercial, and navigational expectations.

On-Page SEO

Heading structures, semantic entities, information gain paragraphs, and clean metadata.

Technical SEO

Log file analysis, canonical loops, robots directives, status codes, and server response headers.

Internal Linking

PageRank equity modeling, click-depth flattening, and contextual anchor architecture.

Content Optimisation

Pruning thin pages, consolidating cannibalized URLs, and building authoritative topical hubs.

Local SEO

Google Business Profile optimization, local citations, map pack ranking, and service area pages.

Google Search Console

Reading crawl stats, index coverage errors, canonical mismatches, and regex query analysis.

Analytics & Measurement

GA4 custom events, conversion funnels, landing page attribution, and Google Tag Manager triggers.

Competitor Research

Reverse-engineering backlink profiles, identifying keyword content gaps, and SERP opportunity scoring.

SEO Audits

Structuring professional client audits using Screaming Frog, Sitebulb, and manual inspections.

AI-Assisted SEO Workflows

Leveraging LLM prompting for schema generation, regex queries, and content structure without hallucination.

Reporting

Building automated Looker Studio executive dashboards that communicate commercial value.

SEO Strategy

Translating business targets into 6-month prioritized sprint roadmaps that engineering teams respect.
