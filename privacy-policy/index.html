6. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

Our website is designed with a lightweight, privacy-conscious footprint. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies, invasive third-party ad pixels, or behavioral retargeting networks.

Cookies used are limited to:

  • Essential Technical Cookies: Necessary to remember user UI preferences (such as dismiss state on promotional alerts or active session state).
  • Aggregated Performance Analytics: Used strictly to measure overall site traffic volume, top visited resources, and device distributions without personal fingerprinting.

You can manage or disable cookies at any time via your browser preferences. Disabling cookies will not degrade your ability to navigate our website.

7. Data Retention & Security Measures

We apply defense-in-depth technical and procedural controls to protect data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction:

Encryption in Transit & at Rest: All web traffic is strictly served over modern HTTPS with TLS encryption. Administrative channels enforce multi-factor authentication (MFA).
Credential Revocation Discipline: Upon project conclusion or retainer termination, all delegated Google Search Console, GA4, and CMS user accounts are promptly logged out, deleted from local keychains, and verified revoked.
Retention Limits: Contact records and invoices are retained only for the duration required by statutory financial regulations. Temporary audit crawl files are purged within 90 days of project sign-off unless otherwise agreed in writing.
