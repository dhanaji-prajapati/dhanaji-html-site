15+ Years on the Front Lines of Search Engine Optimization

I began optimizing web architectures for search engines over fifteen years ago. Since then, I have guided brands through Panda, Penguin, Hummingbird, mobile-first indexing, the Helpful Content system, and today's seismic shift toward semantic AI answer engines.

Throughout my career, I watched the standard agency model consistently fail clients. In typical agencies, seasoned specialists are used as sales collateral during pitches, but the actual day-to-day accounts are handed off to entry-level coordinators juggling 20+ clients. Critical indexing roadblocks go unnoticed, recommendations are reduced to boilerplate checklists, and client founders are left frustrated by account-manager telephone games.

I chose a different path: the independent specialist model. I intentionally run a focused consulting practice where I am 100% on the tools. When you hire me, you get my direct brain, my hands-on code execution, and my personal accountability.

Whether you are an ambitious B2B SaaS platform scaling ARR, an enterprise e-commerce brand managing thousands of catalog facets, or a premier digital agency needing elite white-label delivery, you receive the direct attention of a veteran who has navigated every phase of Google search for 15+ years.
